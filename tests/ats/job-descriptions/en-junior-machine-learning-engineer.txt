Title: Junior Machine Learning Engineer
Join our AI team to design, train and deploy machine learning models that power our security products.

Responsibilities
- Train and evaluate deep learning models with PyTorch.
- Prepare datasets and pipelines with Python, NumPy and Pandas.
- Research new techniques for vulnerability and anomaly detection.
- Package and deploy models with Docker on AWS; version code with Git.

Requirements
- Advanced student or graduate in Computer Engineering, Computer Science or similar.
- Solid knowledge of Python, machine learning and deep learning fundamentals.
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow; scikit-learn is a plus.
- SQL and Linux knowledge.
- Strong problem solving, communication and ability to learn quickly. Fluent English.
